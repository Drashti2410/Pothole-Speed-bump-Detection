Road Maintenance Severity Policy:
- 1-2 potholes detected with confidence > 0.7: LOW priority, schedule inspection within 30 days.
- 3-5 potholes detected: MEDIUM priority, schedule repair within 14 days.
- 6+ potholes or any speed bump misclassified as damage: HIGH priority, dispatch crew within 48 hours.
- Speed bumps detected are informational only, not repair triggers.
